Three connected explorations of my evolving relationship to Christianity. I consider these essays to be my best, most honest writing yet! 

[History is a Love Story](https://makinglight.substack.com/p/history-is-a-love-story?r=7no6d)
A line of thinking continuing from my first [youtube video](https://youtu.be/sGfYtAezcCw) about the meaning of love as divine revelation. Our suffering in history as the crucible for the incubation of higher and deeper love. Christ as the centre of this process.

[Lust and Evolution](https://makinglight.substack.com/p/lust-and-evolution?r=7no6d)
Reckoning with animal instincts in civilization. The whole game has changed; our relationship to our nature must be creative and paradoxical—otherwise it's all cost-benefit analysis to better please ourselves. The goal of spiritual life is to be in harmony with all creation; which in a fallen world includes a ton of sin. Christ on the cross, as usual, provides the blueprint for this task.

[Blessed Are the Peacemakers](https://makinglight.substack.com/p/blessed-are-the-peacemakers)
I talk about Israel and Palestine, and why we keep getting stuck in these loops of vengeance. It's again a matter of our natural instincts being twisted under conditions of unnatural scale. The only way out of these death traps, both in the life of the individual and nation-states, is to follow Christ and earnestly seek the miracle of forgiveness. This is the only possible basis for real peace.
