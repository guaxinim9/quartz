It is easily demonstrated that ours is the most inefficient society to ever exist. This is somewhat surprising since we also idolize efficiency more than any society in history. It is to our machinations as military glory to Sparta, or spiritual self-realization to Vedic India. Yet, we fail spectacularly, for our secret hearts still barbarism rules.

Begin with that high temple of civilization: the airport. Imagine telling our ancestors that we are capable of flight, but every aspect of the experience is a total chore!

Does this have to be the case? Probably. Planes take a lot of gas; it makes sense to stuff as many people on board as you semi-comfortably can. That's efficient. The reduced comfort is a worthy trade-off.
